Building KamaraEMR: a national OpenMRS 3 distribution

Kamara's Ministry of Health has spent five years watching its digital health ecosystem fragment — different facilities running different solutions, implemented by different partners for different donor programs. The 2022–2027 Digital Health Strategy sets out to reverse this. One strategic objective is a national EMR: by 2027, the MoH aims to have a local distribution of OpenMRS 3 ready to deploy to health facilities, beginning with two districts including Rutinda District.

KMOH's Digital Health Unit and a local digital health implementing partner have selected Emmanuel Ochieng — a district-level IT Officer with eight years' experience — to join the technical team adapting OpenMRS 3 and building that local distribution, KamaraEMR.

Emmanuel is the only IT officer for Rutindi District's 14 health facilities. He sets up networks at rural health centres, keeps servers running through load-shedding, trains end users, and resolves failed software upgrades. He knows Docker, MySQL, DHIS2 and Grafana. What he has never done is help build a country distribution from the ground up — and that is exactly what the Ministry has now asked him to do.

The pathway follows the KamaraEMR Technical Working Group (TWG) through that work, course by course — from Emmanuel's first OpenMRS install, through choosing and building the distribution, configuring its metadata, frontend and reports, to keeping it secure and running once Rutindi's facilities go live. Each course ends with a capstone in which the learner does the same work for their own implementation context.

How the Implementer Pathway Works

Must
Complete OpenMRS Fundamentals: Intro to OpenMRS before beginning the Implementer Pathway.
May
Learners may arrive having already completed Intro to OpenMRS 3: Technical Overview, Intro to the OpenMRS Data Model, Intro to the Concept Dictionary, and OpenMRS Concept Management Using OCL.
Must
IP Course 1 and IP Course 2 must both be completed before IP Course 3: Building an OpenMRS Distribution.
Must
If not completed earlier, learners must complete Intro to the OpenMRS Data Model and Intro to the Concept Dictionary before proceeding to IP Course 3.
Recommended
After IP Course 3, complete Courses 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 in that order — recommended but not mandatory.
Any time
IP Courses 8–10 (Tools for Monitoring OpenMRS Performance, Maintenance & Troubleshooting, Data Security) can be completed at any point in the pathway.
